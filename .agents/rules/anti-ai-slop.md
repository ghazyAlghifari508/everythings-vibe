### Quick Invariants:
- Zero neon glow, 3D shadows, emoji in UI chrome, nested pills, or pastel icon blobs.
- No duplicate actions (audit existing controls before adding new buttons).
- Use `<PageHeading>` (`@/components/ui/page-heading`) for canonical light-weight typography.
- Hugeicons standard (`@/components/ui/icon`, 18px stroke 1.5).
